# 📜Preface: Why This Blog Exists

###### WZM | Saturday, 11 July, 2026

Software architecture holds a special place in my heart. Like engineers and architects from other engineering fields, I find blueprints and design diagrams fascinating. However, software architecture is more than just abstract designs or premium code; it is a practical toolbox of proven solutions and patterns for software development task.

Software architecture to me is a practical knowledge. It is much easier to figure out via hands-on implementation rather than by reading textbooks or staring at complex diagrams—something I struggled with during my university software architecture course. That struggle is exactly why I started this blog. Here, I will be sharing my thoughts and discoveries as I learn about software architecture topics through hands-on experience.

### ⚠️ A Short Disclaimer
I am a junior backend software engineer with two years of experience, currently working in the financial industry. **I am not a trained or professional software architect.** I am an amateur on a journey to become one. The articles I write here are self-assessments of my own understanding. They are best used as learning references, and I hope they bring value to anyone else walking this same path.

### 🏃‍♂️ A Note to Self
Last but not least, I hope can I stick with this journey. I know consistency is hard, and there might be times I want to drop it. This is a reminder to myself: **this is not a race, and there are no rewards.** It is just a random journey I chose to begin. I can take a break and resume anytime—but I won't give up.
